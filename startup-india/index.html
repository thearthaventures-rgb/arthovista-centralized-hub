What Is Startup India

Startup India Explained Simply

Startup India is an initiative aimed at supporting the growth of new businesses in India. Through the recognition process, eligible startups are identified by the government and may become eligible for various benefits — subject to prevailing rules.

Recognition is not automatic. It is assessed by the government against current eligibility criteria. That's where ArthoVista helps: we make the process understandable and support you at every step so you can present your business accurately.

ArthoVista is a full-spectrum business advisory. Beyond Startup India, we help enterprises with government schemes, grants, subsidies, private capital access, compliance, and growth strategy — spanning 120+ schemes across 26+ ministries.

Eligibility

Could Your Startup Potentially Qualify?

Potential eligibility depends on the entity, age, turnover, innovation, sector and other requirements applicable under current Startup India / DPIIT guidelines. The following are typical points that are commonly considered.

01

Entity Type

Typically Private Limited companies, LLPs, and registered partnership firms are considered.

02

Age & Turnover

Recency of incorporation and scale of turnover are commonly considered factors, as per current rules.

03

Innovation & Scalability

Work toward innovation, development, improvement, or a scalable business model with growth potential is often relevant.

Not every business qualifies — requirements can change, and each case is assessed individually.

Why Eligible Startups Pursue Recognition

Eligible and recognised startups may become entitled to certain benefits — subject to applicable criteria and prevailing rules. Benefits are never automatic.

Potential Tax Benefits

Eligible recognized startups may qualify for tax-related benefits under applicable provisions, subject to meeting all criteria.

Simplified Compliance

Recognized startups may benefit from reduced compliances and self-certification opportunities where applicable.

Fast-Track IP Support

Recognized startups may be able to access expedited processing and rebates for patents and trademarks, subject to eligibility.

Access to Ecosystem

Recognition may open doors to government schemes, funding channels, and ecosystem support, where you qualify.
